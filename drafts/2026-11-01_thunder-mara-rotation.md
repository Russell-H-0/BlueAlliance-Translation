How can Thunder survive Chet Holmgren's absence? Rookie Aday Mara becomes vital as trade rumors swirl
雷霆怎么熬过没有霍姆格伦的两个月？新秀马拉成了关键，交易流言四起

As a third star will miss significant time due to injury, the Thunder face a challenging road ahead
第三位球星因伤长期缺阵，雷霆前路艰难

The Oklahoma City Thunder announced that Chet Holmgren underwent treatment on Friday to address a small patellar tendon tear in his right knee, an issue that will keep him out of the lineup for the next 8-10 weeks.
俄克拉荷马城雷霆宣布，切特·霍姆格伦已于周五接受治疗，处理右膝一处小型髌腱撕裂，他将因此缺席接下来的8到10周。

With Jalen Williams out for the season following a gruesome leg injury and Cason Wallace set to miss most of the season after surgery to repair a torn labrum in his left shoulder, the Thunder are now without three of their top six players on the roster less than a month into the season.
杰伦·威廉姆斯在遭遇那次触目惊心的腿部伤病后赛季报销，卡森·华莱士在修复左肩盂唇撕裂的手术后预计缺席本赛季大部分时间。赛季开打还不到一个月，雷霆阵容里最好的六名球员已经少了三个。

The Thunder are off to a 4-1 start so far, but a lopsided loss to the Dallas Mavericks on Wednesday night with Holmgren out offered a glimpse into how challenging life will be with another star on the shelf.
雷霆目前4胜1负开局，但周三晚霍姆格伦缺阵的那场对达拉斯独行侠的惨败，已经让人窥见又一位球星挂上伤病名单之后，日子会有多难过。

For a team that began the season as the co-favorites in the Western Conference alongside the San Antonio Spurs with a win total over 60, now they face a challenge maintaining a spot in the top four in the West after losing so much of their expected production two weeks into the season.
这支球队开季时与圣安东尼奥马刺并列西部头号热门，预测胜场超过60场；而赛季才打了两周，在损失了这么多预期产出之后，他们现在要面对的，是如何保住西部前四的位置。

Oklahoma City has been dominant in recent seasons largely due to its tremendous depth, but this summer saw them take the first steps in shedding that due to the perils of the second apron and rising costs of keeping a talented young core together. Gone are three established contributors in Lu Dort, Aaron Wiggins and Isaiah Joe, and while those were understandable cost-cutting measures, they exacerbate the challenge of dealing with serious injuries to key players at all three levels of the floor.
雷霆近几个赛季的统治力，很大程度上来自惊人的阵容深度。但今年夏天，由于第二土豪线的威慑，以及留住这批年轻核心不断上涨的成本，他们迈出了削减深度的第一步：吕冈茨·多尔特、阿隆·威金斯、以赛亚·乔三名成熟轮换离队。这些减支操作本身可以理解，却让球队在篮下、中距离、外线三个层面同时折损核心球员时更加难以应付。

If there's a silver lining, it's that Holmgren's injury will only cost him a couple of months. But with Wallace and Williams out, losing one of the league's best rim protectors will be extremely difficult to deal with. This was already going to be a challenging season for coach Mark Daigneault without two key backcourt stars, but now he has to shuffle his lineups across the board with Holmgren out.
如果说还有一线希望，那就是霍姆格伦这次只会缺席两个月左右。但华莱士和威廉姆斯都不在，再失去联盟最好的护框者之一，对雷霆来说是极难消化的。对主教练马克·戴格诺特而言，少了两个后场核心，这个赛季本来就已经够难；现在霍姆格伦也倒下，他得把整套轮换重新打乱重组。

Time to sink or swim for Aday Mara
阿代·马拉：要么沉下去，要么游起来

The Thunder probably hoped to bring their rookie big man along slowly, but with Holmgren out, the 7-foot-3 center out of Michigan will have to take on a larger spot in the rotation. Mara impressed in spot duty in his debut against Victor Wembanyama on opening night, showing why the Thunder drafted him to combat their chief rival with a rare player with the size and length to bother him -- and alleviate the stress Holmgren faces dealing with Wemby.
雷霆本来大概想让这位新秀内线慢慢成长，但霍姆格伦一缺阵，这位来自密歇根大学、身高2米21的中锋就不得不在轮换里占更大的位置。揭幕战对维克托·文班亚马，马拉在零散的出场时间里表现抢眼，也说明了雷霆为什么选他——用一个身高和臂展都罕见的大个子去对付头号对手，同时减轻霍姆格伦对位文班时的压力。

Mara then picked up his first double-double in a win over the Phoenix Suns on Monday, as he got his first extended burn with Holmgren out, scoring 11 points and pulling down 10 boards in 20 minutes. He was less effective in 21 minutes against the Mavs, and the Thunder will have to navigate the ups and downs of the rookie experience as Mara gets more comfortable in his expanded role.
周一击败菲尼克斯太阳那场，马拉拿到生涯首个两双：霍姆格伦缺阵后他第一次得到大段时间，20分钟里得到11分、抢下10个篮板。而在对独行侠的21分钟里，他的效果就没那么好。随着马拉逐渐适应扩大的角色，雷霆也得陪着这位新秀一起经历成长的起伏。

Offensively, Mara is a play-finisher at the rim. So far this season, 80% of his field goal attempts have come within four feet of the rim and 75% of his attempts have come off of zero dribbles. With Mara on the floor, the offense has to adjust compared to what they can do with the rest of their center rotation. Where Isaiah Hartenstein can be a high post hub and Holmgren and Jaylin Williams can space the floor, Mara has less variability to his game. That doesn't mean he can't be effective, but it does require an adjustment on Oklahoma City's end to put him in the right spots and find ways to still create space for others when he's on the floor.
进攻端，马拉是篮下的终结者。本赛季至今，他80%的出手来自篮下四英尺以内，75%的出手是零运球直接出手。马拉在场时，进攻必须做出调整，才能和球队其他中锋在场时的打法衔接。以赛亚·哈尔滕施泰因能在高位当策应轴心，霍姆格伦和杰林·威廉姆斯能拉开空间，而马拉的技术变化更少。这不是说他不能打得有效，而是雷霆必须主动调整，把他放到正确的位置上，同时想办法在他上场时依然为其他人创造空间。

On defense, Mara gives them a needed rim protector and he's proven to be a high-caliber shot blocker already with seven blocks in five games despite relatively light minutes. However, like many young shot blockers, Mara is learning to navigate an increased minutes load without fouling. He has picked up nine fouls combined in his two games with 20-plus minutes. Defense will be Mara's calling card, and over the next two months, the Thunder will hope to see him continue learning to be impactful without fouling to maximize his time on the floor.
防守端，马拉给了球队急需的护框，而且他已经证明自己是高水平的盖帽手——出场时间不算多，五场球就送出7次盖帽。不过和许多年轻盖帽手一样，马拉正在学着在更长的出场时间里控制犯规：他两场20分钟以上的比赛合计吃到9次犯规。防守会是马拉的招牌，接下来两个月，雷霆希望看到他在不犯规的前提下持续产生影响，把自己的上场时间撑到最长。

The Thunder never viewed this season as one to focus on development, but at this point they have little choice but to embrace that with Mara. He has the potential to be a key contributor to this team and play an extremely valuable role as they get set for a decade-long battle with the Wemby-led Spurs. With Holmgren out, he's going to get far more extended opportunities to see the floor than expected early in his rookie year, and perhaps that can pay dividends down the road for Oklahoma City.
雷霆从没把本赛季当成培养年，但事到如今，在马拉这件事上他们别无选择，只能接受。他有潜力成为这支球队的重要成员，在球队准备与文班领军的马刺展开长达十年的对抗时，扮演极有价值的角色。霍姆格伦缺阵，意味着他在新秀赛季早期就会得到远超预期的大段出场机会，而这或许会在往后给雷霆带来回报。

Will this change OKC's approach to the trade market?
这会改变雷霆对待交易市场的思路吗？

With Williams out for the year and Wallace out for at least most of it, there were plenty of rumblings about the Thunder exploring their options on the trade market. A deal was unlikely to happen until at least December 15, when players who signed this summer become available, but Oklahoma City figured to spend the next month-plus evaluating its roster and keeping an eye on potential additions from around the league to stay in the contender realm.
杰伦·威廉姆斯赛季报销、华莱士至少缺席大部分赛季之后，关于雷霆在交易市场上试探选项的传闻一直不少。任何交易最早也要等到12月15日——那天夏天新签的球员才可以被交易——但雷霆原本的打算，应该是在接下来一个多月里边打边评估阵容，同时留意全联盟可用的补强人选，让自己留在争冠者的行列里。

With Holmgren out, one wonders whether that changes the calculus for Sam Presti -- or at least the timeline for making a move. Holmgren is expected to return in January, which would allow the Thunder to wait until he's back and see how he's performing coming off of the knee procedure for a couple of weeks before the trade deadline.
霍姆格伦再倒下，人们自然要问：这会不会改变萨姆·普雷斯蒂的算盘，或者至少改变他动手的时间表。霍姆格伦预计一月回归，这意味着雷霆可以等他回来，先观察他做完膝盖处理之后两三周的状态，再在交易截止日之前做决定。

The Thunder should be able to manage two-plus months of a center rotation of Hartenstein, Williams and Mara -- with more Kenrich Williams at the four as well -- meaning their focus will still be on adding on the wing and in the backcourt.
未来两个多月，雷霆靠哈尔滕施泰因、杰林·威廉姆斯和马拉三人轮转中锋位置应该撑得住，再加上肯里奇·威廉姆斯更多打四号位——也就是说，他们的补强重点仍然会放在锋线和后场。

Presti may want to see exactly what he has this season before making a significant in-season move. The Thunder could try to weather the storm with the roster they have until Holmgren returns and see where they are in the West before determining whether to make a smaller move or a more foundation-shaking trade.
普雷斯蒂也许想先看清这支球队本赛季到底是什么成色，再动大的。雷霆可以先用手里的阵容硬扛到霍姆格伦回归，看看自己在西部处于什么位次，再决定是做一笔小交易，还是动一笔动摇根基的大交易。

Could Shai Gilgeous-Alexander win a third-straight MVP?
谢伊·吉尔杰斯-亚历山大能拿下三连MVP吗？

No one has won three straight MVPs since Larry Bird in the 80s. For one, it's hard to maintain that level for three straight seasons, but as we've seen over the years, even when the greats do stay in the MVP conversation, voter fatigue typically sets in after a player wins two and voters seek out someone else.
自80年代的拉里·伯德之后，还没有人拿过三连MVP。一来，连续三个赛季维持那种水准本身就难；二来，这些年我们也看到了，即便是历史级的球员一直留在MVP讨论里，拿过两次之后投票疲劳通常也会出现，投票人会去找另一个人。

However, with the injuries piling up in Oklahoma City, if there were ever a year for a player to break that drought and become the first to three-peat as MVP in 40 years, it could be Shai Gilgeous-Alexander dragging this Thunder team to another top-four seed with such a depleted roster.
但雷霆的伤病不断堆积，如果说有哪一年真有人能打破这层荒，成为40年来第一个MVP三连的人，那可能就是谢伊·吉尔杰斯-亚历山大——拖着这样一套残缺的阵容，把这支雷霆带到又一个西部前四。

It's been a relatively slow start to the season for Gilgeous-Alexander, averaging 26.0 points, 4.6 rebounds and 3.4 assists per game on 45.9/26.5/87.5 shooting splits. That kind of production obviously won't get him a third MVP award, but if he ups his level and carries this Thunder squad without Holmgren for half the season and without Williams and Wallace for most of it, he will have to garner real consideration.
吉尔杰斯-亚历山大本赛季开局相对慢热，场均26.0分、4.6个篮板、3.4次助攻，投篮/三分/罚球命中率为45.9/26.5/87.5。这样的产出显然拿不到第三座MVP，但如果他把水准提上去，在半个赛季没有霍姆格伦、大部分赛季没有威廉姆斯和华莱士的情况下扛着这支雷霆往前走，他就必须被认真考虑。

Some of his candidacy will be determined by who else is in the mix early on -- Luka Doncic seems poised to put up massive numbers in L.A. -- but the circumstances in Oklahoma City this season are so unique that Gilgeous-Alexander could break the run of back-to-back MVPs who have fallen short in pursuit of a third straight.
他的竞争力有多大，一部分取决于开局阶段还有谁在竞争——卢卡·东契奇看起来准备在洛杉矶打出爆炸数据——但雷霆本赛季的处境实在太特殊，吉尔杰斯-亚历山大有可能打破「两连MVP追三连必失手」的惯例。

Gilgeous-Alexander's play will determine exactly how the Thunder proceed this season. If he plays at his typical MVP level and the Thunder look like a threat, Presti may have no choice but to try and make a splash ahead of the deadline to bring in reinforcements to chase a second championship. If it's a down year for Gilgeous-Alexander without some of his top support elements around him, perhaps there's less of a push to go all-in this season and Oklahoma City could look to ride out the storm and hope for better health in 2027-28.
吉尔杰斯-亚历山大的表现，将决定雷霆本赛季到底怎么走。如果他打出惯常的MVP水准、雷霆看起来仍是威胁，普雷斯蒂可能别无选择，只能在截止日之前做一笔大动作补强，去追第二座总冠军。如果少了身边这些顶级帮手，他打出一个下滑的赛季，那么本赛季全力一搏的动力或许就没那么强，雷霆可能选择熬过这一季，寄望2027-28赛季更健康。
